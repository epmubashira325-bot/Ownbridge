OwnBridge

A multi-shop online platform that lets several independent online shops run on one system, each with its own products, orders and customers, managed from a common admin dashboard.

OwnBridge is operated by TechFifo.

About

OwnBridge is a brand and platform owned and operated by TechFifo. Designed for distributed commerce architectures, it enables businesses to launch and maintain multiple store storefronts under a unified infrastructure while preserving operational separation across individual product catalogs.

Through its centralized management model, OwnBridge serves organizations that require administrative consolidation across individual store brands, handling isolated shop databases alongside centralized system supervision.

What We Do

Multi-shop Management

Run multiple distinct online storefronts on a single software installation, maintaining separate domain configurations and inventory records for each shop.

Order Handling

Process incoming purchases and view transactions per store, ensuring sales data, shipping records, and fulfillments stay organized by shop.

Customer Communication

Maintain separate customer profiles, records, and contact points specific to each independent shop on the platform.

Central Admin Dashboard

Monitor platform health, manage shop settings, and supervise overall store operations from a single unified interface.
